Bao Cam

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Bao Cam does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking in the app.

Camera

Bao Cam uses the camera to take your photos. Images are processed on your iPhone and never leave it.

Photos

Bao Cam can only add photos to your library, and only when you tap Save. It cannot see your other photos.

Motion

Bao Cam reads your iPhone's motion sensors to show a level and to turn its buttons upright. This stays on your iPhone.

On your iPhone

Your camera settings, film count and whether you've unlocked film are kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The unlock is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; Bao Cam never sees your payment details.
