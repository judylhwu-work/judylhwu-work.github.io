Bao Cam

Support

Bao Cam turns your iPhone into a little instant camera.

Out of film?

Your first three shots are free. After that, tap Unlock to buy unlimited film once ($4.99 in the U.S.).

New phone, or reinstalled the app?

On the film screen, tap Restore purchase to get your unlock back, free.

Can't save to Photos?

Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Bao Cam and allow adding photos.

Black screen?

Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera and turn on Bao Cam.

Prints look different in full view?

In full view, prints are plain full-frame photos. On the camera's screen they get the instant look.

Still stuck?

Email judylhwu.work@gmail.com with “Bao Cam” in the subject line. Include your iPhone model and iOS version.
